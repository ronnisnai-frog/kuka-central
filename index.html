Help & Guide

An overview of what each part of KUKA Central does.

No topics match your search.

Dashboard

A global overview combining all three survey types — total submissions, synced/pending counts, provinces, a weekly submissions chart, review progress, and Team Collaboration showing every surveyor's recent activity.

Survey

Reveals the three survey types — Mud Crab, Coconut Crab Socio-economic, and Coconut Crab Biological. Selecting one scopes the Summary, Users, Data, and Settings tabs to just that survey type.

Calendar

Track real team meetings and events. Add an event with a title, date, time, and duration — it shows on the month grid with a marker, and in the side panel when that day is selected.

Analytics

Combined charts across all survey types — submissions by province, submissions over time, species/catch breakdown, a surveyor leaderboard, and a Synced/Pending/Approved comparison.

Team

Add team members by email, name, and role (Admin or Surveyor). Each card shows real Active/Away status based on recent activity, and a genuine submission count matched to their name.

Notifications

The bell icon (top right) shows a real notification feed — new submissions, team changes, event creation, exports, and status changes. An orange dot means something's unread. Click the bell to view them, and use "Mark all as read" to clear it.

Summary Tab

Shows the selected survey's description, question count, region, and category, plus a Submissions card with counts over 7/31/90/365 days and a running total.

Users Tab

Lists everyone who has requested or been granted access to KUKA Collect, with their approval status and request date.

Data → Table

A full spreadsheet-style view of every submission, one row per survey. Search and filter by province, hide columns you don't need, and select rows to bulk Change Status, Edit top-level fields, Delete, or Export to Excel.

Data → Reports

An automatic frequency breakdown for every field — how many submissions answered it, and the most common values — useful for a quick sense of your data without opening Excel.

Data → Gallery

Every specimen photo captured in the field (Mud Crab and Biological surveys only), labeled with survey ID and date, sortable and filterable by date range. Click any photo to view it full-size.

Data → Map

Plots every GPS-tagged submission for the selected survey type. Markers are color-coded by survey type — orange for Mud Crab, purple for Socio-economic, dark teal for Biological.

Change Status

A review workflow separate from sync status — mark submissions as Approved, Not Approved, On-Hold, or Not Reviewed to track your own data quality checks.

Settings → General

Edit the description shown on a survey's Summary tab. The Danger Zone here permanently deletes all data for the currently selected survey type — export a backup first if there's any chance you'll need it.

Settings → Activity

A real audit log of admin actions — logins, exports, deletions, and description changes — with filtering and pagination.

Settings Page

Update your profile photo, name, and notification/appearance preferences, including a working Dark Mode toggle. Links out to Account Settings for organization info, password, and security.

Account Settings

Update your profile details (name, organization, etc.) under Profile, or your password, email, and login history under Security, including the option to log out of every device at once.

Data & Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

KUKA is a fisheries survey system used by approved surveyors to collect data on mud crab and coconut crab populations in Papua New Guinea. This policy explains what information KUKA collects, why, who can see it, and how it's protected.

Information We Collect

Surveyor account information — full name and email, to create and manage your account.

Field and specimen data — for Mud Crab and Biological surveys: GPS location, date and time, species, sex, measurements, habitat notes, and optional specimen photographs.

Respondent information (Socio-economic Survey only) — full name, age, gender, marital status, village and province, contact details, and answers about livelihood, harvesting, and household circumstances.

Device and location data — GPS provided automatically, entered manually, or selected on a map.

Why We Collect This Information

This data is collected solely to support fisheries stock assessment and coastal community research in Papua New Guinea. It helps the KUKA research team understand crab populations, harvesting patterns, and the livelihoods connected to them, in order to inform sustainable fisheries management.

Respondent Consent

Before recording any Socio-economic survey, the surveyor is required to explain the purpose of the survey to the respondent and obtain their verbal consent. Respondents may decline to participate, or decline to answer any individual question, without consequence. Consent is recorded as part of each submission.

Who Can Access This Information

Surveyors can see and edit their own submitted surveys. Administrators can view, review, export, correct, or delete data across all surveys through KUKA Central. No one outside the KUKA surveyor and admin team has access to this data.

Data Storage & Security

Data is stored using Supabase, a secure cloud database provider, with access controls restricting who can read or modify each record. KUKA Collect also stores unsynced survey data locally on the surveyor's device until it is uploaded.

Data Retention

Survey data is retained for as long as it is needed for research and fisheries management purposes. Administrators can permanently delete individual surveys or entire datasets through KUKA Central when data is no longer needed.

Right to Deletion

A respondent may request that their personal information be removed from KUKA. Requests can be made through the surveyor who conducted the interview, or directly to a KUKA administrator, and will be honored where technically possible.

No Third-Party Sharing

Respondent and survey data is never sold, shared, or made available to third parties outside the KUKA research and administration team, except where required by law.

Specimen Photos

Photographs taken during Mud Crab and Biological surveys are of crab specimens, not people, and are used to verify field measurements and species identification.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as KUKA's features evolve. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

Questions

Questions about this policy or about your data can be directed to your KUKA administrator.
